Bring Ever After High Back

Nostalgia of the Past

Ever After High: a beloved timeless toy line created by Mattel back in May of 2013. A product line of dolls shaped after the sons and daughters of renowned fairytale characters spreading a significant message of choosing your own path and being yourself. It reached the peak of its popularity back in 2013 after its initial release of dolls accompained by its animated series. However, sadly in 2018 Ever After High was discontinued after Disney introduced the movie series Descendants. Descendants was another universe that depicted the lives of fairytale characters' kids but placing a larger emphasis on the children of villians. These two franchises clashed leading to Mattel decreasing the quality of their Ever After High Dolls till eventually discontinuing the line entirely.

Now with the rerelease of Monster High: another toy line by Mattel that focused on children of characters in horror stories we know, Ever After High is making a come back with its current and expanding fan base. Many fans are posting on social media making a call for the return of Ever After High to be produced in the style of the new Monster High dolls. Furthermore, many comments notice Mattel has brought the logo of Ever After High back onto their website. With this increased popularity, Mattel should bring back Ever After High. Not only would it be successful with a guaranteed fan base willing to make purchases, it will help remind older fans of their childhood and create good memories for future fans.
